# Privacy

Download & Open in IINA (yt-dlp) does not collect analytics, telemetry, or
personal data for the developer. It does not send data to a developer server.

When you click the toolbar button or a download context-menu item, the
extension sends the selected page or media URL to its local native messaging
helper. The helper runs yt-dlp, which contacts the requested site and any
services needed to retrieve the media. Those services receive normal network
request information, such as your IP address and the requested URL, under
their own privacy policies.

Downloaded media is saved to your Downloads folder and opened locally in IINA.
The extension does not forward Firefox cookies, passwords, or browser history
to the helper. Error details may appear in Firefox's extension console.
